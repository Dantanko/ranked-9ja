Get your clinic listed

Free, and it never changes your rank.

Listing is free and has no effect on your rank. Every clinic is scored by the same method, and we check each application before it goes live.

What to have ready

  1. Clinic name, address and area of Abuja
  2. Phone and WhatsApp number
  3. Services offered, and prices or price ranges if you publish them
  4. Any specialist services you offer, with the practitioner's specialist qualification
  5. Opening hours
  6. Name and Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria registration details of a practitioner at the clinic
  7. A link to your Google Business Profile, if you have one

No Google profile or website?

You can still be listed. We use your Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp Business page if you have one. If you have nothing online, you are listed as Collecting reviews, and ranked once 10 patients have confirmed their visit with us on WhatsApp. We never choose who reviews you, and we do not accept reviews you send in yourself.

Photos that earn safety points

Send dated photos or short videos of your sterilisation setup, X-ray machine, emergency kit and backup power. Each one you can show earns points. Anything you cannot show earns none.

Apply to be listed

After you apply, we verify the practitioner's registration, then score and publish your clinic. Sponsored spots and paid profiles are separate and never change a ranking.
