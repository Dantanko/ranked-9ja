How we rank dental clinics

The full method, with nothing hidden.

Every clinic is scored out of 100 using the five measures below. The same method applies to every clinic.

MeasurePointsWhat we look at
Public rating40Average of public ratings, adjusted so a clinic with few reviews cannot top one with many
Review volume20How many public reviews the clinic has
Recent reviews15How many of those reviews are recent
Responsiveness10Whether the clinic replies to reviews and enquiries
Transparency15Whether services, prices or price ranges, and opening hours are published

Who gets ranked

A clinic is ranked only if we can verify that a practitioner there is registered with the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria. Clinics that cannot show this are not ranked.

What cannot change a score

Money. Listing is free. Paid profiles and sponsored spots sit outside the ranking and never move a clinic up or down.

Where the data comes from

We link to the public ratings we use and do not copy review text. Each clinic page shows the date we last checked it.
