How the score works

Out of 100 points, in plain steps. Anyone can check the working.

Every clinic gets a score out of 100. We want you to see why one clinic sits above another, so here is exactly how the points are earned. The same rules apply to every clinic.

1. Public rating (up to 20 points)

We take the clinic's average public rating, out of 5, and correct it for how few reviews it may have. We add 20 pretend reviews of 4.0 stars to every clinic, then work out the average again. A clinic with many reviews barely moves. A clinic with only a few moves toward the middle.

Points = corrected rating divided by 5, times 20.

Why: three reviews of 5 stars should not beat three hundred reviews averaging 4.7. With this rule the first scores about 17 points and the second about 19.

2. Number of reviews (up to 10 points)

100 or more reviews: 10. 50 to 99: 8. 20 to 49: 5. 10 to 19: 3. Fewer than 10: 0.

3. Recent reviews (up to 10 points)

We count reviews from the last 6 months. 10 or more: 10. 5 to 9: 7. 1 to 4: 3. None: 0.

4. Responsiveness (up to 15 points)

7 points if the clinic has replied to at least half of its last 10 reviews. 8 points if it answers an enquiry we send it within one working day.

5. Openness (up to 15 points)

5 points for publishing its services. 5 for publishing prices or price ranges. 5 for publishing its address and opening hours.

6. Specialist services (up to 20 points)

5 points for each specialist service, up to 4. Examples are orthodontics (braces), dental implants, root canal treatment, oral surgery, paediatric dentistry and gum care. A specialist service counts only when a practitioner with a recognised specialist qualification provides it and we have verified that.

7. Safety and facilities (up to 10 points)

2 points for each of five checks: a working autoclave with instruments kept in sealed packs; digital X-ray on site; an emergency kit with oxygen and emergency drugs; backup power; and a written treatment plan and price quote given before treatment. A check counts only when we have seen evidence, such as a dated photo or video, or we have visited. A clinic's own claim earns nothing.

Where the ratings come from

We use Google ratings first. If a clinic has no Google profile, we use its Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp Business page, with the same correction. Each clinic page says which source its rating came from. A clinic with nothing online is listed as Collecting reviews and is ranked once 10 verified reviews from any source are in. We confirm our own patient reviews on WhatsApp, allow one per phone number, and never let a clinic choose who is asked.

A worked example

Take an imaginary clinic with a 4.6 average from 30 reviews, 6 of them in the last 6 months. It replies to 7 of its last 10 reviews and answers our enquiry the same day. It publishes its services and opening hours but not its prices. It offers two verified specialist services. We have seen evidence of its autoclave, backup power and written quotes.

Who gets ranked

Only clinics where we can verify that a practitioner is registered with the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria. A clinic that cannot show this is not ranked at all, whatever its other numbers.

What cannot change a score

Money. Listing is free. Paid profiles and sponsored spots sit outside the ranking and never move a clinic up or down.

Where the numbers come from

We link to the public ratings we use and do not copy review text. Every clinic page shows the date we last checked it. The clinics currently shown on the site are samples, and their scores are placeholders.
