Write for us

Rules for guest posts and how features are chosen.

We publish two kinds of posts. We write service features ourselves. Outside contributors write guest posts. Both are labelled, and neither can change a ranking.

Guest posts we want

Practical, specific advice for patients or clinic owners, written by someone who knows the subject. Between 600 and 1,200 words. Examples: how to compare treatment quotes, what a sterilisation check looks like, how to prepare a child for a first visit.

The rules

  1. It must be your own original work, written for us.
  2. Tell us who you are and where you work. Your bio is shown on the post.
  3. Tell us about any relationship with a business you mention, whether paid, owned, family or a client. If there is one, we may decline or add a disclosure.
  4. No adverts. You get one link in your bio, and we mark it as nofollow.
  5. We do not charge for posts and we do not pay for them. You cannot buy a place, and a post never changes a ranking.
  6. We edit for clarity and accuracy, and we can decline any post.

Service features

We choose these ourselves. A clinic needs a high score with evidence we have seen, and we usually visit. Clinics cannot request a feature or pay for one.
