Privacy

What we collect, why, and your choices.

Last updated 8 Oct 2026. This is a first draft and should be reviewed by a lawyer before the site goes live.

ranking9ja ranks businesses using evidence. To do that we collect some information. This page says what, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

What we collect

  • From businesses that apply to be listed: business name, address, phone and WhatsApp number, services, prices, opening hours, and registration details. Most of this is published on the business's page.
  • From patients and customers who give us a review: your name or initials, the date of your visit, what you used the business for, and your phone number. Your phone number is used only to confirm you are a real customer, and it is never published.
  • From people who contact us: your name, email address and message.

Why we collect it

To list and score businesses, to confirm that reviews come from real customers, and to reply when you write to us. We do not use your information for anything else.

Who sees it

Published business details are public. Reviewer phone numbers and contact messages are seen only by the people who run ranking9ja. We do not sell your information. Our forms are run on Google Forms, so Google also processes what you type into them.

How long we keep it

We keep reviewer phone numbers only for as long as needed to confirm the review, and we delete or remove them after that. Business details stay for as long as the business is listed.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. A business can ask us to correct or remove its listing at any time, and we will correct anything shown to be wrong.
