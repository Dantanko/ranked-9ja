How to choose a dentist in Abuja

Eight checks you can make yourself before you book.

Updated 8 Oct 2026. By the ranking9ja team.

The short answer

Choose a dentist by checking eight things you can verify yourself: their registration, who will actually treat you, how instruments are sterilised, whether you get a written plan and price, what happens in an emergency, whether they have X-ray on site, how clearly they answer questions, and how they respond to their reviews. Reviews alone are not enough, because glowing reviews can hide poor safety.

1. Is the dentist registered?

Ask to see the practitioner's registration with the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria. A clinic that cannot show it is not one to choose. We check this for every clinic we rank, and a clinic that fails is not ranked at all.

2. Who will treat you, and are they a specialist for this?

For braces, implants, root canal treatment or surgery, ask who will do the work and what their specialist qualification is. A general dentist can do many things, but some treatments are best done by a specialist.

3. How are the instruments cleaned?

Ask how instruments are sterilised. A good answer mentions an autoclave and instruments kept in sealed packs that are opened in front of you. This matters more for your safety than the look of the waiting room.

4. Will you get a written plan and price before treatment?

You should receive the proposed treatment and its cost in writing before anything starts, including what happens if the plan changes. A clinic that will only give a price once you are in the chair is a warning sign.

5. Is there X-ray on site?

On-site digital X-ray means a clinic can diagnose and plan without sending you elsewhere. It is not essential for every visit, but it is useful for anything beyond a check-up.

6. What happens in an emergency?

Ask whether the clinic keeps an emergency kit with oxygen and emergency drugs, and whether staff know how to use it. Reactions are rare, but when they happen there is no time to look for equipment. Ask about backup power too, since power cuts are common in Abuja.

7. Do they answer clearly?

Send the clinic a message on WhatsApp with a real question. See how quickly it replies and whether the answer makes sense to you. A clinic that explains well before you are a patient usually explains well afterwards.

8. What do reviews say, and how does the clinic respond?

Read the reviews, but also look at whether the clinic replies to them, including the unhappy ones. A clinic that replies politely and fixes problems is showing you how it will treat you.
