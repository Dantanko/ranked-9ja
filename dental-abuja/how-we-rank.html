How the score works

Out of 100, in plain steps. Anyone can check the working.

Every clinic gets a score out of 100. We want you to see why one sits above another, so here is exactly how the points are earned. The same rules apply to every one.

1. Public rating (up to 20 points)

Points = corrected rating divided by 5, times 20. We add 20 pretend reviews of 4.0 stars to every business, then work out the average again, so a business with only a few reviews moves toward the middle.

Three reviews of 5 stars should not beat three hundred reviews averaging 4.7.

2. Number of reviews (up to 10 points)

100 or more: 10. 50 to 99: 8. 20 to 49: 5. 10 to 19: 3. Fewer than 10: 0.

3. Recent reviews (up to 10 points)

10 or more: 10. 5 to 9: 7. 1 to 4: 3. None: 0.

We count reviews from the last 6 months.

4. Responsiveness (up to 15 points)

7 points if the clinic has replied to at least half of its last 10 reviews. 8 points if it answers an enquiry we send within one working day.

5. Openness (up to 15 points)

4 points if it publishes its services. 4 points if it publishes prices or price ranges. 3 points if it publishes its address and opening hours. 4 points if it publishes payment or instalment options for larger treatments.

6. Specialist services (up to 20 points)

5 points for each, up to 4.

Examples are orthodontics (braces), dental implants, root canal treatment, oral surgery, paediatric dentistry and gum care. A service counts only when a practitioner with a recognised specialist qualification provides it and we have verified that.

7. Safety and facilities (up to 10 points)

2 points if it has a working autoclave with instruments kept in sealed packs. 2 points if digital X-ray is on site. 2 points if it keeps an emergency kit with oxygen and emergency drugs. 2 points if it has backup power. 2 points if it gives a written treatment plan and price quote before treatment.

A check counts only when we have seen evidence, such as a dated photo or video, or we have visited. A clinic's own claim earns nothing.

Who gets ranked

Only if all of these are true. Otherwise it is not ranked at all, whatever its other numbers.

  1. We have verified that a practitioner there is registered with the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria.

A business with fewer than 10 verified reviews from any source is listed as Collecting reviews and ranked once it reaches that number. We confirm our own reviews on WhatsApp, allow one per phone number, and never let a business choose who is asked.

What cannot change a score

Money. Listing is free. Paid profiles and sponsored spots sit outside the ranking and never move a business up or down.
